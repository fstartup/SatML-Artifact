## Traceability rules

1. External claims about standards, frameworks, specifications, or published research must use one of the keys above.
2. Proposed constructs introduced by this paper are labeled **Proposed** or **Synthesized** and are not upgraded by citation alone.
3. Deterministic experimental results are supported by the experiment protocols, result files, and evidence matrix in the working repository rather than by external literature.
4. Exploratory model observations are labeled **Exploratory observation** and are not treated as statistically generalizable evidence.
5. The paper does not use citations as substitutes for evidence of its own experimental findings.
